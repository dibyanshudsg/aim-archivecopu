To: Kaushal Ji

Cc: Dibyanshu Ji

Subject: AIM Archive — Current Status and Request for Planned Handover to SAS

Namaste Kaushal Ji,

Warm greetings. As suggested by Dibyanshu Ji, I am writing to share the current position of the AIM Archive project and discuss its future management.

As a brief follow-up to Dibyanshu Ji's earlier feedback, I have expanded the Issues-page search field, reorganised the issue-action layout, and added a dedicated Navigation Management section for managing public website links. The suggested Dynamic Pages Editor has not been implemented and is outside the planned handover scope.

The online catalogue currently contains 104 issues: 102 English All India Magazine issues and two Agnishikha issues. Further Agnishikha uploads are currently on hold, and the present upload focus is on English AIM issues. The remaining English archive coverage and the batch to be completed before handover still need to be agreed.

I have also continued improving reading, search and the admin workflows. The website is available at https://aim-archive.vercel.app/.

I would now like to arrange a formal handover of the project to Sri Aurobindo Society's IT Team. My intention is to complete an agreed preparation and transition process, after which I would step away from ongoing development, hosting administration, maintenance and operational responsibility. I wanted to communicate this clearly in advance so that we can plan the transition together.

Before handing it over, I want to ensure that the receiving team has a reliable system and the information needed to operate it independently. I have reviewed the project and prepared a phased checklist covering the remaining work: strengthening restricted-PDF access, verifying complete backups and restoration, reconciling the source code and deployed version, completing indexing and admin workflow checks, and preparing technical documentation and an admin walkthrough. These checks are still in progress; I am not proposing an immediate transfer of an unverified system.

The website currently uses Vercel for hosting, Turso/libSQL for the database, Cloudflare R2 for PDFs and covers, and GitHub Actions for manual search indexing. The hosting arrangement can be reviewed with your IT team during the handover, taking into account the Society's existing DigitalOcean infrastructure. I have prepared migration options for that discussion; moving the website would not necessarily require changing the database and file storage at the same time.

I plan to coordinate the handover with Dibyanshu Ji, covering the source code, documentation, technical maintenance, archive uploads/content review, and service accounts and billing arrangements. Please let me know if you would prefer someone else to receive the handover or would like any additional team members involved.

Could you please confirm whether the Society can accept the project for ongoing management and guide us on the following?

1. What archive coverage and functionality should we agree to complete before acceptance, and what should continue under the Society's team afterward? This would also clarify the remaining historical uploads and future enhancements discussed earlier.
2. Who should approve the final handover, and what would be a suitable transition period and effective date for transferring responsibility?

I can share the current technical specification, remaining-task checklist and migration notes, and arrange a walkthrough with the designated team. Together, we can agree on a defined handover scope and record completion before concluding my responsibilities.

Over the past two to three months, my office projects and other commitments have limited the time I could devote to the archive. This has made me realise that sustaining it requires consistent institutional support and a team with clear responsibility for its upkeep and development. I remain willing to help with the transition and offer further assistance wherever my availability allows, to the best of my ability, while the Society's team takes responsibility for its ongoing management.

If feasible, it would be especially meaningful to aim for an official launch on 24 November 2026, marking the centenary of Siddhi Day. This is my personal wish, subject to your guidance and our completing the necessary preparation and testing together.

It has been meaningful to contribute to making these publications more accessible. I hope we can bring the archive to a dependable and well-documented state, ready to serve readers and continue growing under the Society's care and the Grace of the Lord and the Mother.

With regards,

Mugdhanil
